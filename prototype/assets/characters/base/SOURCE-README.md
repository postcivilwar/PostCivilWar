# Recording-referenced streetwear character

A custom approximation of the character in your uploaded recording; not the original extracted character. Hidden surfaces and unreadable clothing graphics are inferred/abstracted.

Included: streetwear_character_rigged.glb, front.jpg, back.jpg.

GLB 2.0, embedded textures, approximately 1.8 m tall, 43,130 triangles, 22-bone movable humanoid rig. Idle: four-second looping breath and subtle upper-body weight shift. No walking, facial rig, finger rig, physics or game-engine retarget preset is included.

The human anatomical topology derives from the MakeHuman base.obj, released explicitly as CC0 in its header. The outfit, accessories, skin weighting and idle animation were custom fitted for this reference. Included MakeHuman_CC0_header.txt preserves the attribution/license evidence. Reference appearance is not a grant of rights to any third-party character design or logo.

Import the GLB into Blender or any glTF-compatible engine. Animate Streetwear_Rig's named Hips, Spine, Chest, Neck, Head and paired arm/leg bones; head accessories follow Head. Enable the Idle clip for playback. Viewer scaling does not change the downloadable model's real-world scale.
