# Innoson IVM CNG coach — photo-referenced 3D model

Green-and-white coach with original photographed IVM exterior and seat badges, INNOSON front and rear lettering, blue cabin seating, pleated curtains, overhead racks and cockpit.

- File: innoson_ivm_cng_bus.glb (self-contained glTF 2.0, embedded materials and logo textures)
- Units: metres; approximate body length 11.6 m, width 2.5 m, height 3.8 m including roof HVAC.
- Geometry: 170,704 triangles. Rigid meshes grouped by material for fewer draw calls. No LODs or collision meshes are supplied; adapt these to your engine's performance budget.
- Front steering pivots: Steering_Pivot and Steering_Pivot_Right, yaw about local Y in GLB.
- Wheels: Wheel_Front_Left, Wheel_Front_Right, Wheel_Rear_Left, Wheel_Rear_Right. Rear pivots include dual tyres. Roll about local X.
- Animations: Steering_Sweep and Wheel_Roll (2 seconds each). Both front wheels are steered; each wheel pivot can be animated independently.
- Driver steering wheel is decorative; functional animation applies to road wheels. Doors are closed and not animated. No driving physics.

This is a custom approximation from the three supplied photographs, not manufacturer CAD or a dimensional scan. Rear and hidden surfaces, seating count and dimensions are inferred. The source photos limit logo sharpness; the actual photographed IVM badges are retained rather than substituted. Slight paint-wear texture from the keke models is not applied to this photographed clean green-and-white coach.
